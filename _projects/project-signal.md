**Goal**

Give operations teams a single screen that replaces scattered spreadsheets and chat threads.

**Highlights**

- Designed an incident timeline that keeps teams aligned.
- Built audit trails and role-based permissions for compliance.
- Added smart alerts to surface the next best action.

**Outcome**

Reduced mean time to resolution by 28% and improved post-incident reporting quality.
