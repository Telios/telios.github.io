**Goal**

Validate a new marketplace with a clean onboarding flow, fast search, and safe payments.

**Highlights**

- Implemented a multi-step onboarding flow that improved activation.
- Built a search experience optimized for first-time visitors.
- Integrated payments, payouts, and dispute workflows.

**Outcome**

The beta attracted 150+ early adopters in the first month and informed the next product roadmap.
